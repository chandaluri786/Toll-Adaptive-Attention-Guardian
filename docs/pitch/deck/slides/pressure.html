[Presenter 2 · 40 s] "Here’s one day. Toll keeps a Focus Pressure score from 0 to 100. Your own blocks set the base: a class or meeting is 65, sleep 70, deep work 50, the hour before bed 40. Nothing scheduled is zero. For the first two hours of distraction each day, that’s all that counts. After that, behaviour adds up: scrolling hard adds 15 or 25, opening the same app again and again adds 10, being behind on your plan adds up to 25. A quest you finished in the last hour, like a walk, takes 15 off. At 65 the sky storms, and it only clears once it drops below 60, so it never flickers. Before any storm you get a 90-second "clouds gathering" heads-up, and every day starts fresh at 4 a.m. Key point for the judges: outside the blocks you declare, the score tops out at 60. Toll can’t storm on you at random. And a plan is optional, with no plan, the strictest you ever get outside sleep is Cloudy."
