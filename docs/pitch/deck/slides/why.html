Why they fail

Weak

You can lift them whenever you like.

Narrow

They only count a few kinds of activity.

Rigid

Fixed timers that ignore your real day.

[Presenter 1 · 30 s] "When we looked at why, it came down to three words. Weak: the barrier is always yours to lift. One tap and it is gone, exactly when you are weakest. Narrow: the tracking only counts certain activity types, so real progress you make in the world doesn’t count. Rigid: limits are fixed time intervals, 30 minutes a day, 9 to 5, that never adjust to the day you’re actually having. Your exam week and your Sunday get the same rules."
