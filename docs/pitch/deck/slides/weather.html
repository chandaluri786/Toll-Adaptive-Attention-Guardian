[Presenter 2 · 35 s] "Toll gives your phone weather. You never set it, it follows your day. Clear skies: everything is open, and you get two hours of free time every day, no judgement. Cloudy: distracting apps cost umbrella time, minutes you earned. Storm: banked minutes don’t work; only fresh effort in the real world opens them, right now. Browsers, email and music stay open when it’s cloudy; in a storm they need a quest like everything else. And calls, maps, banking and up to two messaging apps you pick are always open." Mention: users never see words like "blocked" or "failed", just weather.
